Japanese explorer data

Dictionary readings and kana vocabulary are derived from JMdict. Kanji meanings, on/kun readings, stroke counts and school grades are derived from KANJIDIC2. Copyright James William BREEN and the Electronic Dictionary Research and Development Group. Documentation and license statement.

Vocabulary ordering uses direct Japanese entries in wordfreq 3.1.1, copyright 2022 Robyn Speer, together with JMdict commonness and spelling metadata. This is an approximate discovery ranking, not a measured rank for every dictionary word. Multi-token frequency estimates are not used. Wordfreq's data represents usage through approximately 2021.

Wordfreq source acknowledgements include Wikipedia, OpenSubtitles via OPUS, and SUBTLEX by Marc Brysbaert and colleagues (freely available data). See wordfreq's full source and attribution notice.

The derived explorer data is distributed under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0. Changes include vocabulary reordering, spelling filtering for automatic edges, compact reading extraction and kanji vocabulary selection. No endorsement by the data authors is implied.

Character colors use KANJIDIC2’s Mainichi Shimbun newspaper frequency ranks, independently of vocabulary ordering. Fixed bands are top 50, 150, 400, 800, 1,500 and beyond; gray means unranked. These are corpus frequencies, not learning difficulty. KANJIDIC2 JLPT levels are not displayed because they describe the older test system.
